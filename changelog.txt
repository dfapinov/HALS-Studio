11/10/26 - Latest changes

- Stage 4 added Sound Power vs Harmonic Degree, with total and per-degree
  traces versus frequency, Internal/External field selection.

- Order N growth optimisation moved to Stage 4 before the full solve. It uses
  the current Target N Max, including manually entered values, rather than only
  Stage 3 recommendations.  Transitions can delay up to 3x their original entry
  frequency, then activate even if the condition ceiling is unmet so higher
  orders are still considered. The growth explanation and comparison plots
  moved from Stage 3 help to Stage 4 help, with the search limits documented.

- Stage 2 added independent LF/HF hold-coordinate controls, both enabled by
  default, in one Advanced Settings row. End coordinates are held outside the
  tested range; disabling a side exports zero coordinate shift on that side.
  Updated Stage 2 help.

- Progress reporting improved. Stages 1-4 show progress and adaptive ETA in the
  bottom bar, alongside system CPU utilisation.

- The Stage 4 spatial error viewer uses azimuth/elevation (azel) rotation to
  keep roll fixed without changing other 3D viewers.

- CLI panes follow new output only when within three lines of the
  bottom. Earlier text remains in place while users scroll back, including
  across shared views and updating progress lines.

09/10/26 - Earlier fixes

- Stage 2 applies optimized origins only within its selected test range.
  Origins outside that range are saved as zero shift, clearing values from an
  earlier, wider run.

- The order N growth optimiser keeps KR transitions that are already below the
  ceiling (10 times the N=4 reference). Transitions above the ceiling are
  delayed and refined to avoid unnecessary delay, including when a search
  sample falls below the lower target.
